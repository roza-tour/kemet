## What to expect

We want you to feel completely confident before you commit to anything.

### The price you confirm is the price you pay

Every quote includes a full itemisation of what is included and what is not. There are no surprises at check-out.

### Your itinerary, not an adapted template

We don't adapt a standard programme to your dates. We build yours from a blank page.

### A named contact throughout

You deal with one person who knows your trip in detail — not a call centre, not a different agent each time.

### No pressure to decide

We take the time needed to get the itinerary right. There is no artificial deadline on a revised proposal.

### Our own team, our own contracts

Your licensed Egyptologist guide and your driver are Kemet's own team, and the hotels are booked under our own contracts with them, chosen for quality and local ownership.
