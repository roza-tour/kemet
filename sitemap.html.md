## Destinations *(19)*

- [Cairo](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/cairo.html) Egypt's capital on the Nile — medieval Islamic and Coptic quarters, the Egyptian Museum, and the gateway to Giza.
- [Giza](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/giza.html) Home of the Great Pyramid and the Sphinx, on the desert plateau across the Nile from Cairo.
- [Luxor](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/luxor.html) The open-air museum of ancient Thebes — Karnak, Luxor Temple and the royal tombs of the Valley of the Kings.
- [Aswan](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/aswan.html) Egypt's tranquil southern city on the Nile — Nubian culture, the High Dam and the island temple of Philae.
- [Alexandria](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/alexandria.html) Egypt's Mediterranean port founded by Alexander the Great — Greco-Roman ruins and the reborn Library.
- [Hurghada](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/hurghada.html) Egypt's main Red Sea resort coast — warm, clear water over island reefs, and the easiest beach pairing with a Luxor or Nile itinerary.
- [Sharm El Sheikh](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/sharm-el-sheikh.html) Sinai's resort capital between desert mountains and the Red Sea — gateway to Ras Mohammed, the Tiran reefs and Bedouin stargazing nights.
- [The Fayoum](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/fayoum.html) A green depression an hour and a half from Cairo — waterfalls in the desert, a lake full of birdlife, whale fossils, and a village of potters.
- [Abu Simbel](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/abu-simbel.html) Two temples cut into a cliff by Ramesses II on the shore of Lake Nasser — and moved, block by block, in the 1960s.
- [Siwa Oasis](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/siwa.html) A remote oasis near the Libyan border: the Oracle of Amun, a mud-and-salt citadel, springs among the palms and the dunes of the Great Sand Sea.
- [Marsa Matruh & the North Coast](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/marsa-matruh-north-coast.html) Egypt's Mediterranean shore west of Alexandria: white sand, turquoise coves, and the battlefield memorials of El Alamein.
- [Port Said](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/port-said.html) The city at the northern mouth of the Suez Canal — nineteenth-century balconies, ships passing at the end of the street, and a ferry between two continents.
- [Saint Catherine & Mount Sinai](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/saint-catherine.html) A sixth-century monastery at the foot of Mount Sinai, in the high granite mountains of the peninsula — and the climb to the summit for sunrise.
- [Dahab](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/dahab.html) A relaxed town on the Gulf of Aqaba, with reefs a few steps from the shore, the Blue Hole to the north and the Sinai mountains behind.
- [Marsa Alam](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/marsa-alam.html) The quieter southern Red Sea: reefs in fine condition, sea turtles in the bays, and dolphins at Samadai.
- [Bahariya & the White Desert](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/bahariya-white-desert.html) An oasis four to five hours from Cairo, and beyond it the chalk formations of the White Desert — best seen at sunset and from a camp under the stars.
- [Kharga & Dakhla Oases](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/kharga-dakhla-oases.html) The southern oases of the Western Desert: a Persian-period temple, one of the earliest Christian cemeteries, and a medieval town of mud brick.
- [Minya & Middle Egypt](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/minya.html) The stretch of the Nile most journeys skip: the painted tombs of Beni Hasan, Akhenaten's city at Tell el-Amarna, and the necropolis of Tuna el-Gebel.
- [Dendera & Abydos](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/dendera-abydos.html) Two temples north of Luxor that many travellers rank above the famous ones: Hathor's at Dendera, with its ceilings in colour, and Seti I's at Abydos.
