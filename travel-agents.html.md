For the trade

# Your clients, our Egypt

Travel agents and tour operators send us their clients for private journeys in Egypt. We arrange and run everything on the ground, and keep you informed at every step.

What we handle

## Everything on the ground, privately

### Licensed Egyptologists

Private, licensed Egyptologist guides from our own team for every visit, in English, German, Italian, Spanish, French, Russian, Indonesian, Malay, Portuguese, Arabic and Mandarin Chinese.

### Vehicles and drivers

A private air-conditioned vehicle and driver for each party, for transfers, touring days and the long roads to Abu Simbel or the oases.

### Hotels and the Nile

Hotels chosen for the room, Nile cruises and private dahabiyas, domestic flights and the sleeper train — booked, confirmed and checked.

### Arrival and departure

Meet and assist at Cairo airport, fast-track on request, and transfers at every arrival and departure.

### Experiences

Hot-air balloons over Luxor, private feluccas, the food evening in Cairo, sunrise at the pyramids — and private access to monuments by permit.

### Food rules and occasions

Vegetarian, no egg, no onion and garlic, allergies: we brief every hotel and boat before your clients arrive. Birthdays and anniversaries are marked with flowers, a private dinner or a surprise.

## Asked by agents

### Do you offer trade rates?

Yes. Trade rates and payment terms are agreed with each partner. Write to us with a first request, or simply ask for our terms, and we send them.

### Which languages do your guides speak?

Our own Egyptologists guide in English, German, Italian, Spanish, French, Russian, Indonesian, Malay, Portuguese, Arabic and Mandarin Chinese.

### Can you handle private groups?

Yes. Everything we run is private — couples, families, friends travelling together and larger private groups, each with its own guide and vehicle.

### How quickly do you reply?

Within two hours, with a real itinerary and a clear price.

### How can partners pay?

By bank transfer, by card or by PayPal.
