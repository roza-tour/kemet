# Programmes

Egypt beyond the Nile valley, planned the way the rest of our journeys are: your own Egyptologist, your own vehicle, your own boat where there is water. Each is priced on request, because each depends on your dates.

Planned from first to last day.

## Journeys of their own

8 days / 7 nights · priced on request

### [Alexander's Road: Alexandria, El Alamein & Siwa](https://kemet-travel.com/programmes/alexanders-road-siwa.html)

The coast road and the desert beyond it, told in order by one Egyptologist: the city Alexander founded, the war cemeteries of El Alamein, and three nights in Siwa, where he came to ask the oracle.

6 days / 5 nights · priced on request

### [The Islands of the Northern Red Sea](https://kemet-travel.com/programmes/red-sea-islands.html)

Three groups of islands, each from your own boat, each reached before the day boats: Shaab El Erg in the north, Giftun at its opening hour, and the Tobia sandbanks off Safaga.

7 days / 6 nights · priced on request

### [Sinai: Reef, Mountain and Monastery](https://kemet-travel.com/programmes/sinai-reef-mountain-monastery.html)

Sinai whole: the Straits of Tiran by private yacht, Mount Sinai at sunset rather than at 2 a.m., the monastery at opening time from next door, and then Dahab.

To add to the end of a Cairo, Luxor or Nile journey.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
