# Marsa Matruh & the North Coast

Egypt's Mediterranean shore west of Alexandria: white sand, turquoise coves, and the battlefield memorials of El Alamein.

## Overview

West of Alexandria the Mediterranean coast runs for hundreds of kilometres of pale sand and water of a colour people do not expect from Egypt. Marsa Matruh, about three hours along it, sits on a sheltered bay with its best beaches a short drive away: Agiba, a cove under limestone cliffs, and the rock pool known as Cleopatra's Bath. On the way lies El Alamein, where the desert war turned in 1942; the Commonwealth cemetery, the German and Italian memorials and the military museum stand a few kilometres apart beside the road. This is where Egyptians themselves come in summer, and it is also the road to Siwa, which lies about four hours south of Matruh.

## Why visit

- A Mediterranean beach holiday few foreign visitors know Egypt has
- The El Alamein memorials, among the most moving sites of the Second World War
- The natural stop on the way to Siwa

## Highlights

- Agiba Beach, a small cove beneath limestone cliffs
- Cleopatra's Bath, a natural rock pool open to the sea
- The Commonwealth War Cemetery at El Alamein and the German and Italian memorials
- The El Alamein Military Museum
- Rommel's Cave, the field headquarters turned small museum at Matruh

## Things to know

- The season is summer — roughly June to September — the reverse of the Nile Valley. In winter the sea is cold and many hotels close.
- El Alamein is about an hour and a half from Alexandria; Marsa Matruh about three hours.
- Egyptian school holidays fill the coast in July and August; June and September are calmer.
- It pairs naturally with Alexandria before it and Siwa after it.

## Good to know

### When is the best time for Egypt's North Coast?

Summer. From June to September the sea is warm and the breeze keeps the coast far cooler than Cairo or Luxor. It is the one part of Egypt where July and August are the season rather than something to avoid — though they are also the busiest weeks.

### Can El Alamein be visited in a day?

Yes, from Alexandria or on the way west. The Commonwealth cemetery, the museum and the German and Italian memorials are close to one another along the coast road and take half a day together.

### Is Marsa Matruh a good stop on the way to Siwa?

It is the usual one. The road to Siwa leaves the coast at Matruh, so a night or two by the sea breaks a long drive and gives you both of the west's landscapes: the Mediterranean and the oasis.
