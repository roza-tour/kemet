# Minya & Middle Egypt

The stretch of the Nile most journeys skip: the painted tombs of Beni Hasan, Akhenaten's city at Tell el-Amarna, and the necropolis of Tuna el-Gebel.

## Overview

Between Cairo and Luxor lies Middle Egypt, and at its centre the riverside city of Minya, about 245 kilometres south of the capital. Few itineraries stop here, which is the reason to. At Beni Hasan, tombs cut into the cliff for the governors of the Middle Kingdom are painted with wrestlers, hunters, acrobats and craftsmen — daily life four thousand years ago, in rows. At Tell el-Amarna, the pharaoh Akhenaten and Nefertiti built a new capital for a single god, abandoned within a generation of its founding; its tombs and boundary stelae remain on the edge of an empty plain. Further south, Tuna el-Gebel holds the tomb of Petosiris, half Egyptian and half Greek in its carving, and galleries where thousands of mummified ibises and baboons were laid for the god Thoth.

## Highlights

- The painted tombs of Beni Hasan, from the Middle Kingdom

- The tomb of Petosiris and the animal catacombs at Tuna el-Gebel

- The Nile corniche at Minya in the evening

## Things to know

- Minya is about three to three and a half hours from Cairo by road, and is also on the main railway line.
- The sites are spread along both banks; two full days cover them without hurry, one day covers Beni Hasan and Amarna at a brisk pace.
- Tell el-Amarna rewards imagination more than the eye: little of the city itself stands, and an Egyptologist makes the difference.
- Hotels are limited and simple compared with Cairo or Luxor.

## Good to know

### Is Minya worth adding to a journey?

For a first visit of a week, usually not — Cairo, Luxor and Aswan come first. For a longer or second journey, or for anyone drawn to Akhenaten and the Amarna period, it is one of the most rewarding additions in Egypt, and you will share the sites with almost no one.

### What is there to see at Tell el-Amarna?

The rock-cut tombs of Akhenaten's officials, with their reliefs of the royal family under the rays of the sun disc; the royal tomb in its desert valley; and the boundary stelae that marked out the city. Of the city itself, only foundations remain — which is why a good Egyptologist is essential here.

### How do I fit Middle Egypt between Cairo and Luxor?

The simplest way is by road from Cairo with one or two nights in Minya, continuing south afterwards by road or rail. It can also be a two-day excursion from Cairo and back.
