# Egypt Highlights Deluxe

A week that gathers the essentials — Cairo, Alexandria and Luxor — with sleeper-train nights between them.

## What does Egypt Highlights Deluxe include, and what does it cost?

Egypt Highlights Deluxe is a private 7-day, 6-night journey through Cairo, Giza, Alexandria and Luxor, from €1,297 per person for a party of 6 or more, or €1,427 per person for two. It includes a private Egyptologist guide throughout. International flights are not included. A flight version, in place of the two sleeper-train nights, is from €1,440.

## Overview

For travellers with a week to give, this journey distils Egypt to its greatest hits without rushing them. Cairo opens with the Egyptian Museum; a full day on the Mediterranean takes in Alexandria; then the sleeper train carries you to Luxor for the temples of the living and the tombs of the dead. You return north for the Pyramids of Giza, Saqqara and the medieval heart of Islamic Cairo. Private throughout, with your own Egyptologist.

Day **01**

### Cairo & the Egyptian Museum

- Private airport welcome and transfer to your hotel
- Afternoon at the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir
- Evening orientation with your guide

Day **02**

### Alexandria day trip

- Drive to the Mediterranean coast
- Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa, Pompey's Pillar and the Citadel of Qaitbay

Day **03**

### Coptic Cairo & overnight train to Luxor

- Hanging Church and Old Cairo in the morning
- Evening transfer to the station

Day **04**

### Luxor East Bank

- Karnak temple complex and the avenue of sphinxes
- Luxor Temple at the city's heart
- Check in to your Luxor hotel

Day **05**

### West Bank & train to Cairo

- Valley of the Kings and the Mortuary Temple of Hatshepsut

Day **06**

### Giza & Saqqara

- Pyramids of Giza and the Great Sphinx

### Islamic Cairo & departure

- Saladin Citadel and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali
- Khan el-Khalili bazaar
- Private transfer to the airport

## Good to know

### How much walking should I expect?

A comfortable amount — temple complexes such as Karnak involve an hour or two on foot over uneven ground. We pace each day and rest in the heat.

### Are the overnight trains comfortable?

Yes. You have a private sleeper cabin with dinner and breakfast served aboard, so the long transfers happen while you rest.

### Can I extend with a Nile cruise?

Absolutely — many guests add a 3 or 4-night cruise between Aswan and Luxor. Tell us your dates and we will tailor the extension.

### How demanding is Egypt Highlights Deluxe?

The walking on Egypt Highlights Deluxe is moderate, as on any journey built around monuments. Nights: Hotels in Cairo and Luxor, and two nights on the sleeper train — hotels instead on the flight version. By road: A day trip from Cairo to Alexandria and back by road. There are no pre-dawn starts.

### Can we fly instead of taking the sleeper train?

Yes. Egypt Highlights Deluxe is also arranged with domestic flights in place of the two sleeper-train nights, with those nights in hotels instead — from €1,440 per person. Ask for the flight version when you enquire.
