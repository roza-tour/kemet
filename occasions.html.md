# When the trip is the occasion

Some journeys are a holiday. Others are marking something — and that changes what the itinerary should be, not just where it goes.

A proposal is planned backwards from a quarter of an hour. A sixtieth birthday wants fewer sites and better ones. Three generations need days that can fork. A second visit is about the country nobody queues for. These are the occasions we are asked for most, and each one changes the plan in a specific way.
