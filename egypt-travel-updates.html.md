# Egypt travel updates: November 2026

What has changed for a visitor this season, each entry with the date it was last checked.

## What has changed for travellers to Egypt this season?

For the 2026–27 season in Egypt, Grand Egyptian Museum tickets are sold online for a dated entry slot, monument tickets are paid by card, and the Giza plateau is entered from the Fayoum road with buses inside. In Luxor the Winter Palace is closed until July 2027 and the tomb of Amenhotep III has reopened.

## At the monuments

What has changed about getting in, and what is open that was not.

### Grand Egyptian Museum: timed tickets, bought online

Changed

The Grand Egyptian Museum sells tickets through its own ticketing site, for a dated entry slot. The galleries open from 9:00 to 18:00 on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and from 9:00 to 21:00 on Wednesday and Saturday; last entry is one hour before the galleries close. A ticket is good for a single entry and is not transferable. The museum says it is not responsible for tickets bought on other platforms.

**On the day.** Book the slot before the day, and plan lunch inside the complex: once you leave, the ticket does not let you back in.

Checked 21 November 2026 · Official source: [Grand Egyptian Museum ticketing](https://tickets.gem.eg/) · [Egyptian Museum or the Grand Egyptian Museum?](https://kemet-travel.com/compare/egyptian-museum-vs-grand-egyptian-museum.html)

### Monument ticket prices: read them for your date

Changed

Entry prices for foreign visitors are set in Egyptian pounds and were revised more than once during 2026, including at the Grand Egyptian Museum at the start of November. Published figures for the same site currently disagree with one another, so this page prints none. The price that applies is the one shown by the official seller for the day of your visit. Tickets at the major sites and museums are paid by card; cash is no longer taken at the Pyramids, Karnak or the main museums.

**On the day.** Carry a Visa or Mastercard, and treat any price in a guidebook or a blog as a guide only. On a multi-day Kemet journey the site tickets are arranged for you.

Checked 21 November 2026 · Official source: [Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/) · [What a journey costs](https://kemet-travel.com/egypt-tour-cost.html)

### Giza plateau: one entrance, and buses inside

Changed

Since 2025 visitors reach the Pyramids through the gate on the Fayoum road, south of the plateau, in place of the old entrance beside the Mena House. Cars and coaches stop at the visitor centre there, and hop-on, hop-off buses carry visitors between the stops on the plateau. Sellers and animal owners are kept to set areas.

**On the day.** Allow more time for the approach than older itineraries show. With a guide, the entry, the route between the stops and the exit are handled for you.

Checked 21 November 2026 · [Giza](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/giza.html)

### Luxor: the tomb of Amenhotep III is open again

Open

The tomb of Amenhotep III in the Western Valley of the Kings reopened on 4 October 2025, after more than twenty years of conservation. It is one of the largest royal tombs on the West Bank and had been closed to visitors for a generation, so it is missing from most itineraries written before this season.

**On the day.** It sits apart from the main valley, so it needs its own place in a West Bank morning. Ask for it by name when the journey is planned.

Checked 21 November 2026 · [Luxor](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/luxor.html)

## Landmark hotels this season

Two of Upper Egypt's historic hotels are under restoration, and it changes where a journey sleeps.

### Luxor: the Winter Palace is closed until July 2027

Closed

The Winter Palace, Luxor's historic hotel on the East Bank, closed in early 2026 for a complete restoration. It is due to reopen in July 2027 as the Mandarin Oriental Winter Palace, and cannot be booked for the 2026–27 season.

**On the day.** A Luxor stay this season is built on the West Bank or at another East Bank address; the guide below sets out which, and why.

Checked September 2026 · [Where to stay in Egypt](https://kemet-travel.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-egypt.html)

### Aswan: the Old Cataract is open, Palace wing only

Open

The Old Cataract in Aswan has been under Mandarin Oriental management since May 2026 and is open. Guests stay in the historic Palace wing, the original building facing the Nile and Elephantine Island, while the newer Nile wing is renovated until July 2027.

**On the day.** Fewer rooms are on sale than in a normal season, so the Aswan nights are worth fixing early.

Checked September 2026 · [Where to stay in Egypt](https://kemet-travel.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-egypt.html)

## On the calendar for 2027

Fixed and expected dates that shape a journey planned now.

### Ramadan 2027: expected 8 February to 8 March

Ahead

Ramadan follows the lunar calendar. In 2027 it is expected to begin on or about 8 February and end on or about 8 March, with Eid al-Fitr in the days that follow; the exact dates are confirmed in Egypt by the sighting of the moon. Many monuments and museums shorten their afternoon hours during the month.

**On the day.** Days start early and finish before the pre-iftar rush, and the evenings in old Cairo are the best of the year.

Checked 21 November 2026 · [Ramadan in Egypt](https://kemet-travel.com/collections/ramadan-in-egypt.html)

### Abu Simbel: the sun alignment, 22 February 2027

Ahead

Twice a year, on 22 February and 22 October, the rising sun reaches the inner sanctuary of the Great Temple at Abu Simbel. Both mornings draw the largest crowds of the year to the temple, and entry on those two days is ticketed at a higher rate than on ordinary days.

**On the day.** Rooms near the temple and seats on the Aswan flights go first. Decide by the end of the year if you want to be there in February.

Checked 21 November 2026 · [Aswan and Abu Simbel](https://kemet-travel.com/destinations/aswan.html)

### Total solar eclipse over Luxor: 2 August 2027

Ahead

On Monday 2 August 2027 the Moon covers the Sun over Luxor for about six minutes and 22 seconds, from about 13:02 to 13:08 local time. Luxor has a modest number of good rooms, and the nights around the eclipse are the most sought-after of its year.

**On the day.** Plan the stay first and the touring around it. August in Luxor is extremely hot, so the days are built around dawn and evening.

Checked October 2026 · [The 2027 total solar eclipse in Luxor](https://kemet-travel.com/guides/total-solar-eclipse-2027-luxor.html)

## Good to know

### Do I need to book Grand Egyptian Museum tickets in advance?

Yes. The Grand Egyptian Museum sells tickets through its own ticketing site for a dated entry slot, and says it is not responsible for tickets bought elsewhere. The galleries open at 9:00 and close at 18:00, or at 21:00 on Wednesday and Saturday, with last entry an hour before closing. A ticket allows one entry only.

### Is the Winter Palace in Luxor open?

No. The Winter Palace closed in early 2026 for a complete restoration and is due to reopen in July 2027 as the Mandarin Oriental Winter Palace. It cannot be booked for the 2026–27 season. The Old Cataract in Aswan is open, with guests in the historic Palace wing while the Nile wing is renovated.

### When is Ramadan in 2027?

Ramadan in 2027 is expected to run from about 8 February to about 8 March, with Eid al-Fitr in the days after. The dates are confirmed in Egypt by the sighting of the moon, so they can move by a day. Many monuments shorten their afternoon hours during the month.
