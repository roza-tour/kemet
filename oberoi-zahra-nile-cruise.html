The Oberoi Zahra

Twenty-eight cabins and suites on the river between Luxor and Aswan, every one of them facing the water. We book it for you on request, and plan the rest of Egypt around the days you sail.

What is the Oberoi Zahra, and how is it booked?

The Oberoi Zahra is a luxury Nile cruiser operated by Oberoi Hotels & Resorts, sailing between Luxor and Aswan on three-, four- and seven-night itineraries. It has 28 cabins and suites, all facing the river, an 11-metre temperature-controlled pool, a spa and an à la carte restaurant. Kemet books it on request: you send dates, we confirm availability and the rate with the ship in writing, and build the rest of your journey around the sailing.

How it is booked

On request, and in writing

With twenty-eight cabins and suites and fixed departure days, whether there is room is a question about one sailing — and so is the rate. That is why this page carries no price.

01

Your dates

Tell us when, and how many of you. The Zahra leaves on fixed days, so a day either side can decide which sailing is yours.

02

The ship's answer

We ask the ship about that sailing: which cabins and suites are free, and at what rate. You see both in writing. Nothing is held and nothing is charged before that.

03

The journey around it

Cairo before, Abu Simbel or the Red Sea after, the flights and the private transfers between them — one itinerary and one person to call.

04

Your own Egyptologist, if you wish

The ship's visits run in small groups of its own guests. If you would rather have the temples with your party alone, we arrange your own Egyptologist and car for the same stops.

Asked before booking

How much does the Oberoi Zahra cost?

There is no single price to print. The rate depends on the sailing, the season and the cabin or suite, and it is the ship's to quote. We ask for your dates and send the figure in writing, with what it includes, before anything is paid.

Is the ship private?

No. You share the ship with the guests of the other cabins — 28 cabins and suites in all. Your cabin is your own, and in the suites so is the terrace. For a vessel with nobody else aboard, we charter a dahabiya, a sailing boat of a few cabins, on the same stretch of river.

Are the temple visits part of the cruise?

On the ship's published terms, yes: the visits in the itinerary, the guiding and the entrance fees are included, led by the ship's Egyptologists in English, or in other languages with notice. We confirm it in your quote. A private Egyptologist for your party alone can be arranged alongside.

Zahra or Philae — which Oberoi ship?

They are sister ships. The Philae is the smaller: 22 cabins and suites, larger standard cabins at 36 m², and sailings of four or six nights. The Zahra has 28, sails three, four or seven nights, and has the largest suite of the two — 115 m² with its terrace and a private whirlpool. In practice your dates usually decide.

Can children sail?

The ship does not accommodate children under seven, and its policy gives older children a cabin of their own. Tell us the ages when you enquire and we ask the ship what suits your family.

When does it sail?

Through the year. On the ship's published calendars for 2026 and 2027 there are no departures in June. October to April is the comfortable season on the river; from June to August Upper Egypt reaches 40 °C and visits start early in the day.

Can the whole ship be taken for one party?

The ship does not publish a charter. For an occasion we will ask about a specific date on your behalf. Where a party wants the boat entirely to itself, a chartered dahabiya is the certain way.
