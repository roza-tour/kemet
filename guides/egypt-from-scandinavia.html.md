# Egypt from Scandinavia & the Nordics

Planning Egypt from Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland or Iceland: flights, the visa, the time difference, the winter holidays, and how to pair the Red Sea with the Nile.

6 min read · Updated 2026-10

## The dark months are the Nile's best

Egypt's season matches the Nordic winter. From November to March, when the days at home are shortest, Upper Egypt is dry and sunny: 24–26°C by day in Luxor in December, 23–25°C in Aswan in January, 20–23°C in Cairo in February. Evenings are cool — a jacket on the deck after dark, sun cream by day.

By April Luxor reaches 34–37°C, and from May the temples of Upper Egypt become a dawn affair.

## Red Sea charters, with the Nile added

Most winter-sun charters from the Nordics fly to the Red Sea — Hurghada and Marsa Alam, sometimes Sharm el-Sheikh — not to Cairo. In winter the coast is 22–23°C by day in January, with the sea at 23–24°C in December and 21–22°C in February. It is also a good base for the monuments: Luxor is about four hours by road from Hurghada, and Cairo under an hour's flight away.

Three ways to divide the time:

- One week: four or five nights on the coast, then two or three in Luxor with your own Egyptologist — Karnak, the Valley of the Kings, both banks of the river.
- Ten days to two weeks: Cairo and Giza, a flight to Luxor, a three-night Nile cruise to Aswan, then a week by the sea.
- One big day: Luxor as a day trip from Hurghada is possible, but it means about eight hours on the road; a night in Luxor is better if you can spare one.

## The visa

Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish and Icelandic passport holders can apply for Egypt's tourist e-visa online before they fly, on the official portal visa2egypt.gov.eg, or get a visa on arrival. At Hurghada, Marsa Alam and the other Red Sea airports the visa on arrival is a paper sticker, paid in cash and allowing up to 30 days; Cairo has piloted a digital version, paid by card, since 1 August 2026.

The fee changed during 2026 and a further rise has been announced for January 2027: check the current amount on the official portal and pay only there. Your passport should be valid for at least six months beyond your stay.

**Important:** Landing at Sharm el-Sheikh? The free "Sinai only" stamp is for stays of under 15 days in Sharm, Dahab, Nuweiba and Taba. It does not cover Luxor, Cairo or the Nile — for an extension, get the full visa.

## The time difference, country by country

There is no jet lag to speak of from anywhere in the Nordics:

- Sweden, Norway and Denmark — Egypt is one hour ahead for most of the year; for a few weeks in spring, from late March to late April, the clocks are level.
- Finland — the same time as Egypt for most of the year; from late March to late April, Finland is one hour ahead.
- Iceland — Egypt is two hours ahead in winter and three in summer.

## February, Christmas and the autumn break

The February winter-holiday week — sportlov, vinterferie, hiihtoloma — falls in the heart of Egypt's season, with 24–26°C by day in Luxor. The exact week depends on where you live, and families from across Europe travel at the same time: book three to four months ahead.

Christmas and New Year are the single busiest fortnight of Egypt's year. The Stockholm nonstop starts on 18 December 2026, in time for the holidays; for Christmas, book as early as you can.

The October autumn break catches the start of the season: Luxor is still hot, 35–37°C, so the days start early, and the Red Sea is 27–28°C in the water.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there direct flights from Scandinavia to Egypt?

To Cairo, EgyptAir flies nonstop from Copenhagen, and from Stockholm Arlanda from 18 December 2026, with Air Cairo following from 21 December. An Air Cairo nonstop from Oslo is planned from 21 December 2026 — confirm with the airline. Helsinki and Reykjavik are one stop. Winter-sun charters mostly fly to the Red Sea resorts.

### Do Swedes, Norwegians and Danes need a visa for Egypt?

Yes, as do Finns and Icelanders, but it is simple: apply for the tourist e-visa online on visa2egypt.gov.eg before you fly, or get a visa on arrival. Check the current fee on the official portal — it changed during 2026 and a further rise has been announced for January 2027.

### What is the time difference between Scandinavia and Egypt?

Egypt is one hour ahead of Sweden, Norway and Denmark for most of the year, and level for a few weeks in spring. Finland keeps the same time as Egypt most of the year. Iceland is two hours behind Egypt in winter and three in summer.

### Can I combine a Red Sea holiday with Luxor?

Yes. Luxor is about four hours by road from Hurghada, so two or three nights there with your own Egyptologist fit easily into a week on the coast. A day trip is possible, but with about eight hours on the road we suggest staying at least one night.

### When is the best time to visit Egypt from the Nordics?

November to March, when the days at home are shortest: 24–26°C by day in Luxor in December, dry air and clear skies. The February winter-holiday week and Christmas are the busiest weeks, so book early.
