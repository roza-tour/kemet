# Thanksgiving & Spring Break in Egypt

Egypt in an American holiday week: Thanksgiving and spring break both fall in Egypt's best weather. What fits in nine days, the flights, and why to book early.

5 min read · Updated 2026-10

Reviewed by [Mervat Farouk](https://kemet-travel.com/about.html#mervat-farouk) · Egyptologist guide — checked October 2026

## Key takeaways

- Thanksgiving week and spring break both fall in Egypt's best weather
- Nine days, Saturday to Sunday, covers Cairo, Luxor and the Nile
- EgyptAir flies nonstop to Cairo from New York, Newark and Washington Dulles
- Both weeks are among the busiest of Egypt's year: book three to four months ahead

## Thanksgiving week

Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26, 2026. Take the week around it and Egypt is at its most comfortable: 29–31°C (84–88°F) by day in Luxor and 23–26°C (73–79°F) in Cairo, dry air and cool evenings on the deck of a Nile boat. It is the start of Egypt's high season, and the week Americans, Canadians and Europeans all choose at once — the best cabins and rooms go three to four months ahead.

## Spring break

March is one of the very best months in Egypt: 28–31°C (82–88°F) in Luxor and 23–26°C (73–79°F) in Cairo, long days and the light that photographers come for. For families it is the last comfortable window before the heat, and the temples are at their most beautiful in the early-morning light.

## What fits in nine days

From the Saturday before to the Sunday after: three days in Cairo and Giza — the pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Islamic Cairo — then a short flight to Luxor for the Valley of the Kings and Karnak, and either a three-night Nile cruise to Aswan or two nights in Luxor before flying home. We keep the first day light after the transatlantic flight.

## Christmas and New Year

The winter holidays are the other American window — and the single busiest fortnight of Egypt's year. If those are your dates, book as early as you can.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Thanksgiving this year?

Thursday, November 26, 2026. Most travellers take the week around it — nine days from the Saturday before to the Sunday after.

### Is Egypt warm in late November?

Pleasantly: 29–31°C (84–88°F) by day in Luxor and 23–26°C (73–79°F) in Cairo, with cool evenings. It is the start of Egypt's best season.

### Can we see Egypt in a spring break week?

Yes. Nine days covers Cairo and Giza, Luxor and either a short Nile cruise to Aswan or two nights in Luxor — at a comfortable pace, with the first day kept light.

### How early should we book?

Three to four months ahead for Thanksgiving and spring break, earlier for Christmas and New Year — the best Nile cabins and hotel rooms go first.
