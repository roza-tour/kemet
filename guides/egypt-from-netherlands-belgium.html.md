# Egypt from the Netherlands & Belgium

Planning Egypt from the Netherlands, Belgium or Luxembourg: nonstop flights from Amsterdam and Brussels, the visa, the time difference, school holidays, and guiding in English or French.

## Flights from Amsterdam and Brussels

Both capitals have a nonstop flight to Cairo. Frequencies change by season, so check the current schedule when you book.

- Amsterdam Schiphol — EgyptAir daily, and Transavia, nonstop to Cairo in about four and three-quarter to five hours.
- Brussels — EgyptAir nonstop to Cairo; Air Cairo adds flights from 18 December 2026.
- Luxembourg — one stop, or the nonstop from Brussels.

**Note:** Winter-sun charters from the Netherlands mostly fly to Hurghada and Marsa Alam, not Cairo. From Hurghada, Luxor is about four hours by road — we can add the monuments to the beach.

## The visa

Dutch and Belgian passport holders can apply for Egypt's tourist e-visa online before they fly, on the official portal visa2egypt.gov.eg, or get a visa on arrival: a paper sticker, paid in cash and allowing up to 30 days, or at Cairo, where it has been piloted since 1 August 2026, a digital version paid by card. Luxembourg passport holders should check their options on the same portal.

The fee changed during 2026 and a further rise has been announced for January 2027, so check the current amount on the official portal and pay only there. Your passport should be valid for at least six months beyond your stay.

**Important:** Pay for an e-visa only on visa2egypt.gov.eg. Look-alike sites add their own fee.

## The time difference

Egypt is one hour ahead of Amsterdam, Brussels and Luxembourg for most of the year; for a few weeks in spring, from late March to the last Friday of April, the clocks are level. With no jet lag, there is no recovery day to plan in.

## School holidays

Apart from the summer, Dutch and Belgian school holidays fall in or near Egypt's October-to-April season:

- Autumn (herfstvakantie, or the Belgian autumn break around 1 November): 35–37°C in Luxor in October, 29–31°C in November, and the Red Sea still warm enough to swim.
- Christmas: 24–26°C in Luxor and cool evenings — in the single busiest fortnight of Egypt's year. Book as early as you can.
- February (voorjaarsvakantie, krokusvakantie): 24–26°C in Luxor, among the most comfortable weeks of the year for temples.
- Easter and May: 34–37°C in Luxor in April and 38–41°C in May, so temple visits start at dawn. For the meivakantie, Cairo with Alexandria, or the Red Sea, suits the heat better.

**Tip:** In the Netherlands the autumn and February holidays are staggered by region; in Belgium, French- and Dutch-speaking schools have followed different calendars since 2022. ## How long to go for

Five hours from Amsterdam or Brussels makes short trips worthwhile:

- Four days: Cairo and Giza — the pyramids and Sphinx, the Grand Egyptian Museum, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara.
- Nine days, a school holiday week: Cairo, then Luxor — or a three-night Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan. With younger children we shorten the mornings and leave time for the pool.
- Two weeks: Cairo, Luxor, the Nile to Aswan, and a few days on the Red Sea to finish.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there direct flights from the Netherlands and Belgium to Egypt?

Yes. From Amsterdam, EgyptAir flies nonstop to Cairo daily and Transavia also flies the route, in about five hours. From Brussels, EgyptAir flies nonstop, and Air Cairo adds flights from 18 December 2026. Check the current schedule when you book.

### Do Dutch and Belgian citizens need a visa for Egypt?

Yes, but it is simple: apply for the tourist e-visa online on visa2egypt.gov.eg before you fly, or get a visa on arrival. Check the current fee on the official portal — it changed during 2026 and a further rise has been announced for January 2027.

### What is the time difference between the Netherlands and Egypt?

Egypt is one hour ahead of the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg for most of the year, and level for a few weeks in spring, from late March to late April.

### Can we travel with a French-speaking guide?

Yes. We have Egyptologists on our own team who guide in French, and our French site covers planning in French. German-speaking Egyptologists are part of the team too.

### Is Egypt a good idea for the May holiday?

It is hot in Upper Egypt in May — 38–41°C in Luxor — so temple visits start at dawn. Cairo with Alexandria, or the Red Sea, suits the week better; for Luxor and the Nile, the autumn, Christmas and February holidays are more comfortable.
