# The decisions people get stuck on

The questions we are asked more than any others, answered with the verdict first and the reasoning after — including the times the answer is the cheaper option.

## Nile cruise or land tour between Luxor and Aswan?

For the Luxor–Aswan stretch, take the Nile cruise. The temples on that section — Kom Ombo, Edfu and Esna — sit on the riverbank and were built to be approached from the water. A cruise also removes two or three hotel changes, because the accommodation moves while you stay put.

Checked by [Mervat Farouk](https://kemet-travel.com/about.html#mervat-farouk), Egyptologist guide
