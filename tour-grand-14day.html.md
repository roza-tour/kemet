# The Grand Tour of Egypt

Fourteen days, every register of the country — Cairo, Alexandria, the Nile by cruise, Abu Simbel and the Red Sea to finish.

## What does The Grand Tour of Egypt include, and what does it cost?

The Grand Tour of Egypt is a private 14-day, 13-night journey through Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Aswan, Abu Simbel, Luxor and Sharm El Sheikh, from €2,728 per person for a party of 6 or more, or €3,001 per person for two. It includes a private Egyptologist guide throughout. International flights are not included.

## Overview

This is the itinerary for travellers who intend to come once and see it whole. The first ten days belong to history: the Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum, Cairo's medieval and Coptic quarters, Alexandria on the Mediterranean, then a flight south for Abu Simbel — the mountain temple of Ramesses II above Lake Nasser — before a three-night cruise carries you down the Nile from Aswan to Luxor. The last four days change key entirely: after Luxor's tombs and temples you fly to Sharm El Sheikh, where the desert meets some of the clearest water on earth. Three nights on the Red Sea close the journey with snorkelling over the reefs of Ras Mohammed and nothing on the schedule that cannot be cancelled in favour of the pool. All domestic flights are included; every guided day is private. The order of the days is set once your dates are fixed: Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, so we arrange the days around the flights and the opening times of each visit.

## Good to know

### Is fourteen days too long for a first visit?

It is the length Egypt needs to be seen whole rather than sampled. The Red Sea days at the end are what make the history week sustainable — the trip breathes instead of accumulating fatigue.

### How much of this journey is flying?

Three internal flights, each about an hour, replacing what would otherwise be twelve-hour road or rail legs. Two long drives stay on the road: the day trip to Alexandria, and Aswan to Abu Simbel and back — the second is itself part of the experience, crossing open desert at dawn.

### Can the Red Sea leg be swapped or extended?

Freely. Some guests trade it for extra Luxor nights; others extend Sharm to a full week, or add a Siwa chapter with extra days — Siwa is a day's drive each way, so it does not fit inside the three Red Sea nights. The first ten days form the spine and the final act is yours to shape.

### How demanding is The Grand Tour of Egypt?

The walking on The Grand Tour of Egypt is moderate, as on any journey built around monuments.
