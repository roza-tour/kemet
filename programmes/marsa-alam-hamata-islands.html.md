# The Far South: Marsa Alam and the Hamata Islands

Turtles at first light, and islands with nothing on them. The desert road from the Nile to the far south of the Red Sea, where the coast is still quiet.

Length
5 days / 4 nights
Route
Luxor (or Edfu) to Marsa Alam
Best months
March to May, and September to November; winter is warmer here than further north
Use it as
An extension to a longer journey
Price
On request

**For:** Travellers who have had enough of people, and want turtles, dolphins and islands with no beach club on them.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### The desert road
  
  Luxor to the coast by the Edfu–Marsa Alam road, about five hours (three hours forty-five from Edfu, if you are leaving a Nile cruise).
2. Day 2
  
  ### Abu Dabbab
  
  At first light, before the day buses: green turtles grazing on the seagrass, seen with a private snorkel guide. The afternoon is free.
3. Day 3
  
  ### The Qulaan islands
  
  South to Hamata, about an hour and a half by road, then out by boat to the Qulaan islands — small white-sand islands inside the national park — for the lagoons, the reef and, on the way home, the mangroves.
4. Day 4
  
  ### Samadai, or the wadi
  
  The dolphin reef of Samadai if a place within the daily quota can be had; otherwise Wadi el-Gemal with an Ababda guide, and a dark, quiet night to finish.
5. Day 5
  
  ### Departure
  
  From Marsa Alam airport, or by road to Hurghada (about three and a half hours).

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** Flights out of Marsa Alam run on limited days, and the island boats and Samadai work to park quotas; both set the order of the days.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- Islands with nothing on them — the opposite of Giftun: no concession, no loungers.
- The coast as the end of a journey: you arrive by the desert road from the Nile, not by charter flight.

### What is private

Vehicle and driver; the snorkel guide; the boat on day 3.

### Where you sleep

Marsa Alam has few hotels at the standard of the rest of our catalogue. We choose a small, quiet one and describe it frankly.

**When not to go.** June to August is very hot.

## Asked before booking

### Are the islands open to landing?

They lie inside a national park and the rules decide where a boat may land, especially in the months turtles nest. We plan the boat day to what is permitted on your dates and tell you before you go.

### How do I leave?

By road to Hurghada, or by air from Marsa Alam on the days it runs — flights are limited, which is why the last day is the last thing we fix.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
