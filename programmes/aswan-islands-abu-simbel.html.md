# Aswan: Islands of the First Cataract, and a Night at Abu Simbel

Aswan told as an archipelago, five islands in two days by water — then Abu Simbel in the late afternoon and again at sunrise, with a night beside the lake between.

Length
4 days / 3 nights
Route
Aswan to Aswan
Best months
October to March. Around 22 February and 22 October, rooms at Abu Simbel sell out months ahead.
Use it as
An extension to a longer journey
Price
On request

**For:** Travellers who would otherwise give Aswan one rushed day and Abu Simbel a four o'clock start.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Elephantine and the islands
  
  A private motorboat to Elephantine: the ruins of ancient Abu, the Nilometer, the small museum, the Nubian lanes. Sunset under sail around Kitchener's Island.
2. Day 2
  
  ### Upriver
  
  To Sehel for the Famine Stela and the cataract inscriptions; then Philae on its island, with Bigeh across the channel. Lunch on Heissa.
3. Day 3
  
  ### To Abu Simbel
  
  South by road, three to three and a half hours, or by air in forty-five minutes. The temples in the late afternoon, when the day visitors have gone; the sound and light show; a night beside the lake.
4. Day 4
  
  ### The temples at sunrise
  
  The temples again in the morning light, then back to Aswan and onward.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** Flights to Abu Simbel run on set days and times; by road it is a pre-dawn start. We choose between them on your dates, and the days are placed accordingly.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- Aswan as an archipelago: five islands in two days, by water.
- Abu Simbel twice, in the two good lights, instead of once in the morning with every other day visitor.

### What is private

Your Egyptologist; the motorboat and a felucca; vehicle and driver.

### Where you sleep

Aswan in the hotels our catalogue already uses. Abu Simbel has few hotels, and none matches the best in Cairo or Luxor: the night is simple, and good for its view and its hour.

**When not to go.** May to September.

## Asked before booking

### Is this the same as the Abu Simbel day trip?

No. The day trip is an early start from Aswan and a few hours at the temples with everyone else. This one sleeps at Abu Simbel, so you see the temples at the end of one day and the start of the next.

### When does the sun reach the inner sanctuary?

Twice a year, on 22 February and 22 October, the rising sun reaches the sanctuary and lights three of its four seated statues. Those dates sell out months ahead, so tell us early if they matter.

### How is the order of the days decided?
