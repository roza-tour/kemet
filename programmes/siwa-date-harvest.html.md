# Siwa at the Date Harvest

Four nights timed to the weeks the dates are picked and the oasis gathers for its autumn full-moon festival — and a way in that can skip the long road.

Length
5 days / 4 nights
Route
Cairo to Cairo, by road
Best months
Mid-October to mid-November, for the harvest; outside the harvest the same shape runs from October to April
Use it as
An extension to a longer journey
Price
On request

**For:** Travellers adding one remote chapter to a Cairo or Nile journey, and those who like to arrive when something is happening.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Cairo to El Alamein
  
  About three hours by road. The memorials in the afternoon; a night by the sea.
2. Day 2
  
  ### On to Siwa
  
  South across the desert, about six to six and a half hours in all. Sunset at Fatnas.
3. Day 3
  
  ### Harvest day
  
  A morning in the date gardens with a Siwan grower — the climb, the sorting, the varieties; an olive press; the Oracle and Gebel al-Mawta in the late light. On festival days, an hour with a Siwan host at Gebel Dakrour, if the community welcomes visitors that year.
4. Day 4
  
  ### Salt and sand
  
  The salt lakes at first light; the Great Sand Sea and Bir Wahed in the afternoon, by 4x4 with a permit we arrange.
5. Day 5
  
  ### Home
  
  Siwa to Cairo in one long day of eight to ten hours — or add a night on the coast.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** The harvest and the festival follow the season and the moon, and Siwa has no scheduled flights. Both set the days.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- It is dated. The days fall in the weeks the gardens are busiest and the autumn gathering takes place — the festival follows the moon, so we set the days once we know the year's dates.
- A faster way in, where it can be had: a private aircraft from Cairo can replace the road on the first two and the last day, as the airstrip is military and landing needs permission, which takes about two weeks. We tell you whether it is available for your dates.

### What is private

Your guide, vehicle and driver throughout; the grower's garden is visited by your party only.

### Where you sleep

A small lodge in the oasis, simple by design; the coast night is in a hotel by the sea.

**When not to go.** June to August.

## Asked before booking

### Are foreign visitors welcome at the festival?

It is a gathering of the Siwan community, not a performance. Whether and how visitors are welcomed varies by year, so we ask a Siwan host before we promise it, and we plan the harvest day to be worth the journey either way.

### Can I fly instead of driving?

A private charter is possible on request. Siwa's airstrip is military, landing needs permission (about two weeks), and the aircraft cannot stay overnight, so we confirm it for your dates before we plan around it.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
