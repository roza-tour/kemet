# Mount Sinai at Sunset

The mountain and the monastery without a night on a coach: up in the afternoon, the summit at sunset, and Saint Catherine's Monastery at opening time the next morning.

Length
2 days / 1 night
Route
From and back to Sharm El Sheikh or Dahab
Best months
March to May, and late September to November
Use it as
An add-on
Price
On request

**For:** Guests already staying in Sharm or Dahab who want Sinai's interior.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Up the mountain
  
  Leave Sharm at 08:00 and reach St Catherine by late morning. Lunch and rest; then up the camel path in the early afternoon with a Bedouin guide, the summit at sunset, and down by torchlight — about three hours up and two down.
2. Day 2
  
  ### The monastery
  
  Saint Catherine's Monastery at opening time with your guide, then back to Sharm by mid-afternoon, or to Dahab by lunch.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** The monastery is closed on Fridays, Sundays and Greek Orthodox feast days, so day 2 cannot fall on one. If you are diving, we place it before the dive days, or after a full day out of the water.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- The hour of the climb and the hour of the monastery: the same two points as the longer Sinai programme, as a single overnight.

### What is private

Your guide, vehicle and driver; the mountain guide.

### Where you sleep

Simple, and said to be: St Catherine has no hotel at the standard of the rest of our catalogue.

**When not to go.** December to February (below freezing at the summit after dark); June to August.
