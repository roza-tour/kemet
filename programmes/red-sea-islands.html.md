# The Islands of the Northern Red Sea

Three groups of islands, each from your own boat, each reached before the day boats: Shaab El Erg in the north, Giftun at its opening hour, and the Tobia sandbanks off Safaga.

Length
6 days / 5 nights
Route
Hurghada airport to Hurghada airport
Best months
April to May, and September to November (November is usually the calmest)
Use it as
A journey of its own
Price
On request

**For:** Travellers who want the sea itself — swimmers, snorkellers, families with confident children — rather than a pool with a view of it.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Arrive; El Gouna
  
  Hurghada airport and a private transfer to El Gouna, about thirty to forty minutes.
2. Day 2
  
  ### North
  
  Your own boat from El Gouna to the reefs of Shaab El Erg — dolphins sometimes come, if they choose — and the islands beyond.
3. Day 3
  
  ### A slow morning
  
  A morning with nowhere to be, then south by road to Sahl Hasheesh, about an hour.
4. Day 4
  
  ### The centre: Giftun
  
  Out at 07:00 for the first mooring of the day on the island's reef; ashore when the island opens, at nine, and away before the day boats land. Lunch on board in the lee of Abu Minqar or Magawish; back by mid-afternoon.
5. Day 5
  
  ### South: Tobia
  
  From the Soma Bay pier to the Tobia sandbanks off Safaga, early — or a day ashore.
6. Day 6
  
  ### Home
  
  A private transfer to the airport, twenty minutes.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** The national park decides where a boat may moor and land, and the wind decides whether it goes at all. Sea days are placed with a spare day where we can and moved around the forecast.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- First out, first ashore, gone before the fleet. Day boats leave together in the morning and put guests ashore in batches; a private boat can leave at first light and be there as the island opens.
- Three island groups, not one. Giftun is sold as if it were the only island on this coast.

### What is private

The boat, its crew and a marine guide on every sea day; all transfers.

### Where you sleep

Beach resorts at El Gouna and Sahl Hasheesh or Soma Bay, at the standard of the rest of our catalogue. A night on an island itself is not part of this programme: stays on islands on this coast are few, and we say what is available on your dates if you ask.

**When not to go.** June to August (strong wind, and heat); December to February (cool water, and wind can cancel a day on the boat).

## Asked before booking

### What if the wind cancels a boat day?

It happens, most often from December to February and in high summer. We place the three sea days with room to move one, and rearrange them around the forecast rather than cancel them.

### Can we sleep on an island?

Not by default. Island stays on this coast are few; if you want one, ask, and we will tell you what exists on your dates and whether it belongs in the programme.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
