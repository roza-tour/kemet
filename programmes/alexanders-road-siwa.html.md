# Alexander's Road: Alexandria, El Alamein & Siwa

The coast road and the desert beyond it, told in order by one Egyptologist: the city Alexander founded, the war cemeteries of El Alamein, and three nights in Siwa, where he came to ask the oracle.

Length
8 days / 7 nights
Route
Cairo to Cairo
Best months
Mid-October to November, and February to March
Use it as
A journey of its own
Price

**For:** Travellers who have seen the Nile and want the Egypt that lies beyond it.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Cairo to Alexandria
  
  North by road, about two and a half to three hours. The afternoon is with your Egyptologist in the city Alexander founded: the Greco-Roman Museum, Kom el-Dikka, and the corniche at dusk.
2. Day 2
  
  ### El Alamein
  
  Along the coast to the Commonwealth war cemetery, the museum, and the German and Italian memorials. The afternoon is by the sea.
3. Day 3
  
  ### West, then south to Siwa
  
  On along the Mediterranean to Marsa Matruh and south across the desert — about four hours from Matruh. You arrive in time for the light on the lake.
4. Day 4
  
  ### The oasis
  
  The Temple of the Oracle at Aghurmi in the morning; Cleopatra's Spring; the painted tombs of Gebel al-Mawta; the mud-and-salt town of Shali at dusk.
5. Day 5
  
  ### Salt and sand
  
  The salt lakes early. In the afternoon, by 4x4 into the Great Sand Sea: the dunes, a cold lake, the spring at Bir Wahed, and sunset from the crest. Desert days need permits, which we arrange — tell us your dates a few weeks ahead.
6. Day 6
  
  ### A day left empty on purpose
  
  The gardens, an olive or date press at work in season, Fatnas at sunset — and a second visit to the Oracle at the end of the day, in the late light.
7. Day 7
  
  ### Back to the sea
  
  North across the desert and along the coast to El Alamein.
8. Day 8
  
  ### To Cairo
  
  About three hours to Cairo and the airport.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** Siwa is reached by road only: there are no scheduled flights, and the drive from Cairo is a full day. The journey is built around the road, not around the airlines.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- One guide, one story. The route is Alexander's own in 331 BCE — the new city, the coast road west, then south to the Oracle — told in order by the same Egyptologist. Elsewhere the road is only a transfer.
- A third day in Siwa with nothing booked, and a return to the Oracle at dusk instead of a single mid-morning stop.

### What is private

Your Egyptologist, your vehicle and driver from Cairo to Cairo; a licensed Siwan desert driver and 4x4 on the sand day.

### Where you sleep

Alexandria and the coast by the sea. In Siwa, a small lodge in the oasis — the best of them are rustic by design, some without electricity, and we tell you which before you choose.

**When not to go.** June to August (the heat, and the oasis's best lodges have no air conditioning). December and January are workable with warm clothes; we check which coastal hotels are open.

## Asked before booking

### How long is the drive to Siwa?

From Cairo it is a full day — eight to ten hours. This journey breaks it at Alexandria and El Alamein, so the road is part of the story, and the last leg from Marsa Matruh is about four hours.

### Can it be shorter?

Yes. 'Siwa at the Date Harvest' keeps four nights, and in the cooler months a shorter Siwa stay of three nights runs from Cairo by road. Tell us how many days you have.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
