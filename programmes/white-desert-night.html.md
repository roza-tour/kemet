# A Night in the White Desert

One night under a sky with no light in it, among chalk shaped by the wind — with an Egyptologist as well as a desert crew, and a second night in a real bed.

Length
3 days / 2 nights
Route
Cairo to Cairo
Best months
October to April
Use it as
An extension to a longer journey
Price
On request

**For:** Travellers with two spare days in Cairo.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Bahariya, the Black Desert, the White Desert
  
  Cairo to Bahariya, about four hours and a quarter. The gilded mummies at Bawiti with your Egyptologist. After lunch, by 4x4 through the Black Desert, past Crystal Mountain and the cliffs of Agabat, to the White Desert for sunset — and a private camp among the chalk.
2. Day 2
  
  ### Sunrise on foot
  
  Dawn on foot among the formations. Then back to the oasis: the palm gardens, a hot spring, and a night in a lodge.
3. Day 3
  
  ### To Cairo
  
  Bahariya to Cairo, four to five hours.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** Desert permits need weeks of notice and an official guide, and camping rules in the protected area change; we confirm both when we fix the dates.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- A camp that is yours, set before you arrive and with proper bedding — and the second night in a bed with a bathroom.
- An Egyptologist as well as a desert crew. Desert trips are usually sold with a driver-guide only.

### What is private

Your Egyptologist; vehicle and driver; the 4x4 crew and the camp, pitched for your party alone.

### Where you sleep

A private camp, and a lodge in Bahariya, which is simple. Whether camping inside the protected area is allowed on your dates is something we confirm before you book; if it is not, the night is placed in a lodge at the edge of the desert and the evening is spent in the formations.

**When not to go.** May to September; December to February nights are close to freezing in the open desert.

## Asked before booking

### Is it cold?

At night, yes: from December to February close to freezing in the open desert. We supply the bedding; bring warm layers for the evening.

### Do I need a permit?

Desert days need permits, which we arrange. They require weeks of notice, so tell us your dates a month ahead if you can.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
