# Dendera to the Sea

The last great temple on the way to the sea. Luxor to the Red Sea with Dendera guided on the road, a boat at seven in the morning, and a day left open.

Length
4 days / 3 nights
Route
Luxor to the Hurghada coast
Best months
March to May, and September to November
Use it as
An extension to a longer journey
Price
On request

**For:** Couples and families ending a Nile journey who want rest — and one last great temple on the way to it.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Dendera, then the desert road
  
  Leave Luxor at 08:00 for Dendera: two hours with your Egyptologist under the painted ceilings, in the crypt and on the roof, as access allows on the day. Then east through Qena and across the Eastern Desert to the coast, arriving mid-afternoon.
2. Day 2
  
  ### The sea before the fleet
  
  Your own boat at 07:00 and the reef with a marine guide while the water is flat, back by early afternoon.
3. Day 3
  
  ### An open day, three ways
  
  Mons Claudianus — a Roman quarry and fort in the desert behind the coast — by 4x4 with your guide; a first kite or wing lesson on Soma Bay's flat water; or a desert evening with a telescope.
4. Day 4
  
  ### To the airport
  
  A private transfer to Hurghada airport, about twenty minutes from Sahl Hasheesh.

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** Dendera and the quarry road have their own hours and conditions, and flights out of Hurghada decide the last day; the days are placed around them.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- The transfer is a day of the journey. Luxor to the Red Sea is usually a taxi; here it begins at Dendera.
- Rome in the desert: the imperial quarries behind the coast are rarely part of a Red Sea stay.

### What is private

Your Egyptologist, vehicle and driver on day 1; the boat on day 2; the 4x4 on day 3.

### Where you sleep

A beach resort on the coast between Sahl Hasheesh and Soma Bay, or El Gouna if you want a town to walk in; the hotels are at the standard of the rest of our catalogue.

**When not to go.** June to August is hot and windy; December to February is sunny but breezy, with a cool sea.

## Asked before booking

### How long is the drive from Luxor to the Red Sea?

About four hours without a stop. With Dendera on the way the day is longer, and it is a proper day: the temple takes two hours.

### Is Mons Claudianus easy to reach?

It is a half to full day by 4x4 from the coast, on tracks that need the right permits and a driver who knows them. We arrange both, and keep the day free so it can move with the weather.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
