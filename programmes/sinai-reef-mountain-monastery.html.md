# Sinai: Reef, Mountain and Monastery

Sinai whole: the Straits of Tiran by private yacht, Mount Sinai at sunset rather than at 2 a.m., the monastery at opening time from next door, and then Dahab.

Length
7 days / 6 nights
Route
Sharm El Sheikh to Sharm El Sheikh
Best months
March to May, and late September to November
Use it as
A journey of its own
Price
On request

**For:** Travellers who want the sea, the summit and the oldest working monastery — and will give up one night of resort comfort for it.

The plan

## Day by day

1. Day 1
  
  ### Arrive in Sharm El Sheikh
  
  About an hour's flight from Cairo.
2. Day 2
  
  ### The Straits of Tiran
  
  By private yacht: Gordon Reef for an easy first swim, Jackson for confident swimmers, lunch aboard. A reef day — boats do not land on Tiran — and snorkelling only, because tomorrow is altitude.
3. Day 3
  
  ### Mount Sinai at sunset
  
  Inland to St Catherine, about three hours. Lunch and rest; then the long path up the mountain with a Bedouin guide of the Jebeliya, timed for the summit at sunset — two and a half to three hours up — and down by torchlight. A night at the foot of the mountain.
4. Day 4
  
  ### The monastery, then Dahab
  
  Saint Catherine's Monastery at opening time with your guide: the basilica, the mosaic of the Transfiguration, the icons. Then to the Gulf of Aqaba at Dahab, about two hours.
5. Day 5
  
  ### Dahab
  
  The rim of the Blue Hole early, before the day visitors; the lagoon in the afternoon.
6. Day 6
  
  ### Back to Sharm
  
  About an hour by road, with a free afternoon.
7. Day 7
  
  ### Departure

### How the days are ordered

The days below are in their natural order, not their final one. Egypt's domestic flights do not run every day on every route, boats stop when the wind rises, and each site keeps its own hours — so once your dates are fixed we rearrange the days around the flights and the opening times that fall on them.

**What sets the order here.** The monastery is closed on Fridays, Sundays and Greek Orthodox feast days, so day 4 cannot fall on one; the whole week is placed around that, and around the days Cairo, Luxor and Sharm are linked by flights.

What makes it different

## Not the usual version

- Sunset, not sunrise. The usual way up is a night coach and a start at one or two in the morning with every other group. A private afternoon climb puts you on the summit at the end of the day.
- The monastery at opening time, from next door. Day visitors arrive after three hours on the road and share a short opening; you will have slept nearby.

### What is private

Your guide, vehicle and driver throughout; the yacht; the mountain guide.

### Where you sleep

Sharm and Dahab at the standard of the rest of our catalogue. St Catherine has no hotel of that standard, and we say so: the night at the foot of the mountain is in the best simple lodging there is.

**When not to go.** December to February (the summit is below freezing after dark); June to August (too hot on the lower path by day).

## Asked before booking

### Is the climb difficult?

It is steady rather than technical, and a real mountain walk: two and a half to three hours up, cold at the top at any time of year, with the descent after dark. It suits anyone who walks well, and it is not for everyone.

### Can I dive on this programme?

Not in the days before the mountain: altitude follows a dive day badly, and the rule of at least 24 hours before flying applies as well. Dahab is the place to dive, and we place the days so that the buffer is kept.

### What does it cost?

No price is printed here, on purpose. The rate depends on your dates, on what is open and available then, and on the size of your party. Send us the dates and we reply in writing, within two hours, with the route in its final order and the price.
