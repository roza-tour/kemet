Destinations (8)

  • CairoEgypt's capital on the Nile — medieval Islamic and Coptic quarters, the Egyptian Museum, and the gateway to Giza.
  • GizaHome of the Great Pyramid and the Sphinx, on the desert plateau across the Nile from Cairo.
  • LuxorThe open-air museum of ancient Thebes — Karnak, Luxor Temple and the royal tombs of the Valley of the Kings.
  • AswanEgypt's tranquil southern city on the Nile — Nubian culture, the High Dam and the island temple of Philae.
  • AlexandriaEgypt's Mediterranean port founded by Alexander the Great — Greco-Roman ruins and the reborn Library.
  • HurghadaEgypt's main Red Sea resort coast — warm, clear water over island reefs, and the easiest beach pairing with a Luxor or Nile itinerary.
  • Sharm El SheikhSinai's resort capital between desert mountains and the Red Sea — gateway to Ras Mohammed, the Tiran reefs and Bedouin stargazing nights.
  • The FayoumA green depression an hour from Cairo — waterfalls in the desert, a lake full of birdlife, whale fossils, and a village of potters.
